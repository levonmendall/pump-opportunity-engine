Set `include_empty_intervals=true` to fill gaps (OHLC = previous close, volume = 0). **Historical Access by Plan:** * **Basic:** past 6 months * **[Analyst and above](https://www.coingecko.com/en/api/pricing):** September 2021 to present (depending on pool tracking start) **Cache / Update Frequency:**
