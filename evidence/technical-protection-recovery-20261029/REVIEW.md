# Versioned technical-protection recovery producer

This is controlled implementation evidence, not installed/live acceptance. Historical protection breaches remain financial/operational evidence and are not erased by successful recovery.

The existing private trader hard latch remains closed after startup, a protection failure, and proof production. The new producer accepts only the Ledger's separate bounded authenticated technical-control authority; it cannot manufacture that authority or treat an owner/unknown financial stop as technical. Ledger recomputes that authority and binds its strict operating-financial resume to the current account, inception, revision, control history and exact inventory.

For held inventory, progress advances only after the mandatory trader-held-evaluation record returns and the original actual-size quote remains current. Every required holding must complete a genuinely new quote evaluation in the same inventory/context; cached quote reuse and proof polling do not advance the clock. A full 60-second interval, at least 16 complete evaluations, no gap greater than 4 seconds, current reconciliation, USD, bank expiry, financial/evidence safety and controller-reported source/monitor/backup health are required. Any new pause, incomplete context, source failure, pending action, changed inventory, changed authority or time regression resets progress. The final original quote must still be at most 2 seconds old.

If the earlier holding has legitimately closed, the empty-inventory mode explicitly counts new durable cash reconciliations, not nonexistent liquidation/protection checks. It still requires the full new healthy interval, no pending action and a final original USD quote at most 2 seconds old. Changing from held to flat resets the complete interval.

Issued proofs and inventory are frozen and tracked by process-local object identity. Proofs never survive restart or clone/replay as completion authority. Producing a proof does not change the ledger or flags. Only after the strict financial-resume transaction and required readiness record succeed can technicalProtectionRecoveryCommitted clear the private technical latch; it requires exactly revision+1, the expected strict resume control reason, unchanged inventory, no owner stop/loss/pending action, current financial/evidence safety and a still-current proof. An owner stop arriving after resume is preserved. A delayed post-resume confirmation closes entries again. Replaying an already consumed completion creates no new pause and no financial action.

The caller must pass controller health false on unsafe phases, use the Ledger's current technicalProtectionRecoveryAuthority, and continue requiring a new complete independently audited candidate window, fresh executable entry quote and normal per-order risk controls. This producer alone does not authorize any trade or clear unknown financial/evidence failures.

The test suite uses the actual PaperTrader and disposable Ledger commits, independently authored synthetic actual-size financial marks, and the separate new Ledger authority/resume implementation from the general-readiness worktree. No operating financial database, provider or service was accessed. The Ledger overlay is a compile/test dependency only and is not committed by this branch.

Independent review found and corrected a producer API gap: the NativePaper caller recorded recovery evidence, but the producer itself did not originally authenticate that record. The producer now maintains a private recorded-proof WeakSet, marked only after successful mandatory recording of the exact issued proof object, correct strict financial-readiness version, successful resume result, explicit financial-only/no-signal/no-quote/no-order claims and current post-resume CAS conditions. Missing, copied or misclassified records cannot clear the private latch, even through direct API misuse. The expanded 67-test trader/execution suite passes, including all 17 recovery cases.
